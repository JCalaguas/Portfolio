02 · Projects

CS thesis · Team

Driver Drowsiness Detection

The problem
Drowsy drivers rarely notice they are drifting off, and many alert systems depend on an internet connection.
What I did
Helped design an offline three-part system: a USB camera, a laptop running the detector, and an Android phone that sounds the alarm. I trained the eye classifier (EyeCNN) from scratch in PyTorch on 190,490 eye images from the YawDD and NTHU-DD datasets, and split the data by video so the model couldn't cheat by seeing the same driver twice.
  • 87.3%subject-independent accuracy
  • 86.21%precision
  • 5.54%false-alarm rate
  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • OpenCV
  • MediaPipe

Intelligent Systems · Team

Speech Command Recognition with RL Threshold Tuning

The problem
A fixed 0.5 confidence threshold is a guess. Too low and the system acts on wrong commands, too high and it ignores real ones.
What I did
Worked on the speech module: turning WAV audio into Mel spectrograms with librosa, the CNN classifier, and connecting it to a Q-learning agent.
Result
The agent learns the threshold from data instead of guessing, and the project ends with a per-speaker fairness check.
  • Python
  • TensorFlow
  • librosa
  • Q-learning
